## Scope

This repository is designed for roles including:

AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, Generative AI Engineer, LLM Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Agentic AI Engineer, AI Forward Deployed Engineer, FDSE, AI Solutions Engineer, AI Solutions Architect, AI Customer Engineer, AI Technical Consultant, AI Product Manager, AI Engineering Manager, AI/ML Technical Lead, Head of AI, VP of AI, AI Transformation Leader, Chief AI Officer, and Chief Data & AI Officer.

## Interview Philosophy

Do not memorize answers word-for-word. Practice explaining the reasoning behind the answer, the trade-offs involved, the failure modes you would anticipate, how you would measure success, and how you would connect the technical decision to a real business or customer outcome.
